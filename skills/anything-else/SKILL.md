---
name: anything-else
description: Check for loose ends near the end of a session.
---

Review the user's requests, agreed follow-ups, and work performed in this session.

- Identify unfinished work, missing verification, and decisions still needed.
- Check temporary artifacts, changed settings, and running processes created
  for this work. Determine what to keep, restore, stop, or remove.
- Check that useful results and necessary continuation context will remain
  accessible after the conversation ends.

Verify relevant state where practical. Distinguish confirmed completion
from assumptions or checks that could not be performed.

Complete already-authorized finishing work. A status question alone does
not authorize changes. Preserve unrelated work and useful artifacts.

Briefly report remaining items, cleanup performed, and anything deliberately
retained. If nothing needs attention, say so without recapping the session.
